## One ambiguity worth fixing

**Medley's Auto Care has four Louisville locations** — Shelbyville Rd, Outer
Loop, Shepherdsville Rd and S Campbell St, each with its own number. The number
in the app is the **St Matthews branch on Shelbyville Rd**, which is correct,
but the app does not say which branch it is. Someone calling from the south end
of the city may be phoning the wrong side of town.

## A finding that supports the premise rather than undermining it

Three of the Lexington prospects — **Cockrell's Collision, Complete Automotive
Repair and 1st Stop Auto Care** — publish service lists that **do not include
auto glass or windshield work**. Cockrell's does collision, body and paint;
the other two are general mechanical.

Read quickly that looks like bad targeting. It is the opposite. A collision
shop that repairs and paints but does not do glass is precisely the shop that
needs someone to send glass work to. The absence of glass on their service list
is the reason to call, and worth saying out loud when calling.

## Link rot

Every external URL in the app was requested on 2026-09-21. All resolve except:

- **`commercialcafe.com` listings (3)** — 403 to automated requests. These are
  live commercial inventory and are the fastest-rotting content in the app. A
  bay shown as available may have been leased weeks ago. **Confirm by phone
  before driving anywhere.**
- **AI service links** (`chatgpt.com`, `claude.ai`) — 403 to automated
  requests, which is bot filtering, not a dead service.

The two AAA shop-directory links resolve, but AAA's directory returned results
for an unrelated region when queried directly, so the shop details above were
confirmed from other sources rather than from those links.

## What this file does not cover

Opening hours, current pricing, whether a shop is taking on subcontractors,
whether a workforce programme has intake capacity, and whether any listed bay
is still available. **All of that changes faster than this file.**

Verified contacts mean the number reaches the organisation. They do not mean
the organisation can help today.

**Re-check before relying on any of it.**
